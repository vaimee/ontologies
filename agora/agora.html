This ontology designed for farmers to monitor and optimize irrigation procedures and, in the future, other activities related to precision agriculture.

This document is a work in progress

Introduction

"This ontology manages the relationship between weather observations, crop growth parameters, and soil horizons for precision irrigation." "Implementation note: a Protege plugin should be developed to visualize and edit the finite-state machines of the software agents."
